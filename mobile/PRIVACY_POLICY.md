# Privacy Policy for NAV-TIN

**Last Updated:** January 28, 2026

## Introduction

NAV-TIN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Nine Men's Morris mobile game application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide
- **Guest Profile Data**: Username, avatar selection, and theme preferences
- **Game Statistics**: Win/loss records, game history (stored locally and in our database)
- **Online Multiplayer**: Room codes and match data when playing online

### 2. Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Game sessions, features used, app performance metrics
- **Analytics**: Aggregated gameplay statistics for improving user experience

### 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

**Supabase (Database & Authentication)**
- Purpose: Store user profiles, game data, and enable online multiplayer
- Data: User ID, username, game statistics, match history
- Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

**Google AdMob (Advertising)**
- Purpose: Display advertisements to support free gameplay
- Data: Advertising ID, device information, usage patterns
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Note: We implement App Tracking Transparency (ATT) on iOS

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain game functionality
- Enable online multiplayer features
- Track game statistics and leaderboards
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Display relevant advertisements
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Game preferences and offline data stored on your device
- **Cloud Storage**: User profiles and online game data stored securely on Supabase servers
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Retention**: We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services

## Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update or correct your information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Opt-Out**: Disable personalized advertising (via device settings)
- **Data Portability**: Export your game data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@navtin.game

## Children's Privacy

NAV-TIN is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Advertising and Tracking

### iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
On iOS devices, we request your permission before tracking your activity across other apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can change this preference in your device settings.

### Ad Personalization
- You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings
- iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking
- Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending an in-app notification for significant changes
