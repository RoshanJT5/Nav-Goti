🎯 **GAME RULES**
Nine Men's Morris is a classic strategy board game dating back to the Roman Empire. The objective is simple but mastering it takes skill:

1. **Placing Phase** - Take turns placing your 9 pieces on the board
2. **Moving Phase** - Slide pieces to adjacent positions along the lines
3. **Form Mills** - Get 3 pieces in a row to remove an opponent's piece
4. **Win** - Reduce your opponent to 2 pieces or block all their moves
